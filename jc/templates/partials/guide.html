Welcome to Justice Connect

This short guide shows how to use the site. The court registers your case, and this platform helps you find the right lawyer safely.

1. Open your case and give consent

Your cases are listed in My Cases. Open a case and give your consent so verified lawyers can see it. Your phone number and address stay hidden.

2. Chat with lawyers in safe mode

Lawyers send you requests. You can chat, send files and photos, and check each lawyer's rating and solved cases.

  • Phone numbers and emails are hidden until the final acceptance.
  • Select one lawyer. The lawyer then gives the final acceptance or declines.

3. Follow the progress

The progress bar and timeline show every step of your case. Deadlines set by the court appear on your dashboard and in notifications.

  • If your lawyer asks to leave the case, you can allow or decline. No answer means the withdrawal happens automatically.

4. Your tools

The three-dot menu (top right) has language, dark mode, account settings and log out. Use Top Lawyers to see the best rated lawyers. After your case is solved you can rate your lawyer and delete your account if you wish.

{% elif user.role == 'lawyer' %}

Welcome to Justice Connect

Find court-registered cases whose clients have given their consent, and build your track record.

1. Browse and request

Open Browse Cases, read the details and send a request with a short message. The client's identity stays private at this stage.

2. Chat in safe mode

Talk to the client, send files and photos. Contact details are hidden until you give the final acceptance. If the client seems suspicious you can decline.

3. Final acceptance and withdrawal

When the client selects you, give the final acceptance. If you must leave a running case, send a withdrawal request with a reason. If the client does not answer in time it happens automatically.

4. Solve cases, earn reviews

Mark the case as solved. Your solved cases and client ratings appear on your public profile. Update your photo and details in Account Settings.

{% else %}

Welcome to Justice Connect

Register cases and follow them until a lawyer solves them.

Register a case

Enter the case and the client details. The client account is created automatically. For sensitive cases, untick "Notify the client now" and notify later.

Set deadlines

On a case page add deadlines such as hearings or document submission. The client and lawyer can see them and get a reminder.

Follow every case

Manage Cases lists all cases with their progress. Open one to see its timeline, requests and assigned lawyer.
