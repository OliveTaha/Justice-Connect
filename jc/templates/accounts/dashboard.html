Know your rights

Constitution of Bangladesh

Fundamental rights in Part III (Articles 27–44).

Art. 27Equality before law

All citizens are equal before law and are entitled to equal protection of law.

Art. 31Right to protection of law

Every person has the right to enjoy the protection of the law and to be treated only in accordance with law.

Art. 33Safeguards on arrest

A person who is arrested must be told the grounds of arrest as soon as possible, may consult and be defended by a lawyer of their choice, and must be brought before a magistrate within 24 hours (not counting travel time). Special exceptions exist, for example under preventive detention laws.

Art. 35Fair trial

A person accused of a crime has the right to a speedy and public trial by an independent and impartial court. Torture and cruel, inhuman or degrading punishment are prohibited.

Art. 43Privacy

Every citizen has the right to be secure in their home against entry, search and seizure, and to privacy of correspondence and communication, subject to reasonable legal restrictions.

Art. 44Going to court for your rights

You can move the High Court Division of the Supreme Court to enforce your fundamental rights.

General information only, not legal advice. Official laws: bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd
